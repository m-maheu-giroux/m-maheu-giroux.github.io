Mathieu Maheu-Giroux, ScD

Assistant Professor, McGill University

I am an interdisciplinary public health researcher and my research has focused primarily on 1) infectious disease modeling, 2) epidemiology and measurements, and 3) impact evaluations of public health interventions.

As of 2017, I have joined the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at McGill Univeristy. Prior to this appointment, I was a sponsored researcher in the Department of Infectious Disease Epidemiology at Imperial College London. My research program is funded by a FRQS Junior 1 Research Scholar Award.
